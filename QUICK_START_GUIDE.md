## 🛍️ How to Use Each Marketplace

### Bee Colony Marketplace
**Find and purchase premium bee colonies**

1. **Browse:** View all 6 available colonies
2. **Filter:** 
   - By breed (Italian, Rock Bee, Apis Cerana, etc.)
   - By price (₹5,000 - ₹10,000)
   - By location
   - Sort by rating or popularity
3. **Search:** Type colony name or breed
4. **View Details:** Click "Details" button for full information
5. **Add to Cart:** Click "Add to Cart" button
6. **Wishlist:** Click heart icon to save for later

## 🛒 Shopping Features

### Shopping Cart
- **View Cart:** Click cart icon (top right) to see items
- **Add Items:** Click "Add to Cart" on any product
- **Remove Items:** Click "Added" button to remove
- **Cart Counter:** Shows number of items in cart

### Wishlist
- **Add to Wishlist:** Click heart icon on product card
- **Remove from Wishlist:** Click filled heart icon
- **Visual Indicator:** Heart fills with red when added

### Product Details Modal
- **Open:** Click "Details" button on any product
- **View:** Full product information, specifications, benefits
- **Close:** Click X button or click outside modal
- **Add to Cart:** From modal or product card

## 📞 Support

### Common Issues

**Q: Cart not showing items?**
A: Refresh the page. Cart data is stored in browser memory.

**Q: Filters not working?**
A: Click "Filters" button to open filter panel.

**Q: Product details not loading?**
A: Click "Details" button again or refresh page.

**Q: Mobile layout looks wrong?**
A: Ensure browser is in portrait mode for mobile view.
