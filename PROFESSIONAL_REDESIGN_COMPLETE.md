## ✨ Animation Specifications

### Entrance Animations
- Hero: 0.8s fade-in with y-offset
- Headlines: 0.6s fade-in with x-offset
- Cards: Staggered 0.6s each
- Stats: 0.6s staggered entrance

### Hover Animations
- Service Cards: y -12px, shadow enhancement
- Step Cards: y -8px, border color change
- Testimonials: y -8px, border highlight
- Icons: scale 1.1-1.15, rotate 5-8°

### Continuous Animations
- Background Orbs: 8-20s oscillation
- Sparkles Icon: 3s rotation
- Arrow CTA: 1.5s oscillation
